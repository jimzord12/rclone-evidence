Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

rclone-evidence is a personal tool used only by its developer, on the developer's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: only the Google Drive files the tool itself creates (Google scope drive.file). It cannot see any other file in the Drive.
  • What it does with them: uploads test evidence from the developer's computer and creates share links to those files.
  • What it stores or shares: nothing. There is no server; no data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone. Sign-in tokens stay on the developer's own computer.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
